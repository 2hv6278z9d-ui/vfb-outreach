Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

AO Heritage is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of circumstance or ability.

AOHeritage.com is owned and operated by Heritage Group LLC.

We aim to adhere as closely as possible to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities and help make websites more user-friendly for everyone.

While AO Heritage strives to adhere to applicable accessibility guidelines and standards, it may not always be possible to do so in every area of the Website. We are continually working to improve the accessibility and usability of AOHeritage.com.

Due to the dynamic nature of the Website and ongoing updates, minor accessibility issues may occasionally occur. We are continually seeking solutions that will improve accessibility across all areas of the Website and provide a better experience for all visitors.
