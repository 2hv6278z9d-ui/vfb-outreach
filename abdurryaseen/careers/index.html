01 / About

Globe Life is the official insurance partner of

Protecting families. Building leaders.

AO Heritage is part of the AO division of Globe Life — the largest distribution system of a company that's been on the NYSE for over 120 years, survived 3 recessions, and grown through every one. Globe Life is the official insurance partner of the Los Angeles Lakers, the Dallas Cowboys, the Atlanta Braves, FC Dallas, and home to Globe Life Field — the stadium of the Texas Rangers. We work with American Income Life to provide permanent, portable benefits to members of over 45,000 unions and associations across North America.

When members leave their job, retire, or get laid off, they lose their employer benefits within 31 days. We make sure they don't lose the coverage that matters most. We meet with families 100% virtually via Zoom, working by appointment only with qualified leads provided at no cost to our agents.

But what truly sets AO Heritage apart is how we develop people. From day one, every agent is paired with a mentor, trained through a proven system, and supported by a leadership team that's invested in their success — not just their numbers.
