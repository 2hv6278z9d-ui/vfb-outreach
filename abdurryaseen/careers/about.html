01 / Who We Are

A movement, not just an agency

AO Heritage was founded by Torian Fields in Tampa, Florida with a clear vision: build a team of driven professionals who protect families and develop into leaders. What started as a mission to redefine what an insurance agency could be has grown into one of the fastest-growing organizations within Globe Life.

From day one, AO Heritage has been built on authentic connection, servant leadership, and relentless support for every person who joins the journey. Every agent gets a mentor, a proven system, and a leadership team that's invested in their success — not just their numbers.

"AO Heritage isn't just an agency — it's a movement. A place where people find direction, purpose, and the tools they need to build the life they envision."

03 / Our Network

The companies behind AO Heritage

AO Globe Life

Established in 1982, AO is the largest distribution system of Globe Life. AO provides training and support for its Associates throughout North America, with more than 1,800 licensed affiliates across 60 territories.

American Income Life

Principal subsidiary of Globe Life and one of the nation's largest providers of supplemental life insurance to credit unions, labor unions, and associations.

Globe Life Inc.

Founded in 1900, Globe Life is an NYSE publicly-traded company specializing in life and supplemental health insurance for middle-income North Americans.
